The co-op difference

Why a co-op?

A cooperative is owned by the people who do the work. Ownership, decisions, and profits are shared — so no one carries the risk alone, and everyone has a real stake in whether it succeeds.

Shared ownership

Members own the business together and have an equal voice in how it's run — one member, one vote.

Lower risk

Costs, resources, and responsibility are carried together instead of resting on one person or family.

Everyone invested

When ownership is shared, everyone is genuinely invested in the work — and that's what makes co-ops thrive.
